# Sentinel-Weave — Adaptive Defensive Combat Framework

Welcome to **Sentinel-Weave**, a next-generation client-side defensive combat framework built for environments where reaction speed, spatial awareness, and tactical integrity decide the outcome of every encounter. Inspired by the philosophy of lightweight defensive routines but engineered from the ground up as an independent project, Sentinel-Weave reimagines what a guard-first combat companion can be.

Where other utilities focus on brute force, Sentinel-Weave focuses on *grace under pressure*. It is the tactical embroidery that keeps a fighter's defense seamless — woven into every frame, every input, every parry window. Think of it less as a tool and more as a sixth sense for your avatar.

The year is 2026, and combat environments have evolved. Sentinel-Weave evolves with them.

## 🛡️ Overview

Sentinel-Weave is an adaptive, client-side defensive combat framework designed for modern sandbox combat titles. It emphasizes *reactive defense* over offensive automation, providing layered protection through a customizable parry matrix, emergency equipment retrieval, and full attack authority delegation.

The project's core philosophy is simple: **a fighter who cannot be hit cannot be beaten.** Every subsystem in Sentinel-Weave exists to reinforce that principle — from the omnidirectional parry lattice to the sub-frame emergency equip routine.

Unlike heavyweight offensive scripts that draw attention and drain performance, Sentinel-Weave is engineered to be whisper-light. It runs lean, respects your frame budget, and stays out of your way until the moment it is needed.

## ✨ Feature Highlights

### 🔄 360-Degree Parry Lattice
The signature capability of Sentinel-Weave is its omnidirectional parry lattice — a defensive ring that monitors incoming threats from every angle of approach. Whether the strike comes from the front, the flank, or directly behind, the lattice calculates intercept windows and executes parries with sub-frame precision.

- Adaptive arc detection across all 360 degrees
- Configurable parry tolerance windows
- Directional priority weighting for multi-threat scenarios
- Graceful degradation under latency spikes

### ⚡ Sub-Frame Emergency Equip Routine
When defense fails and offense must answer, Sentinel-Weave triggers the emergency equip routine. This subroutine retrieves and deploys the optimal defensive armament within a 14.8-stud radius, prioritizing speed over style. The result is a fighter who is never caught empty-handed.

- 14.8-stud effective retrieval radius
- Priority-based weapon selection heuristics
- Pre-emptive readiness state management
- Zero-input activation pathways

### 🎯 Full Attack Authority
Sentinel-Weave does not shy away from offensive responsibility. The Full Attack Authority subsystem grants the user complete command over their offensive output, ensuring that when the moment to strike arrives, the response is immediate, accurate, and decisive.

- Granular attack timing controls
- Swing commitment management
- Combo flow preservation
- Stance-aware offensive gating

### 🧩 Modular Architecture
Every subsystem in Sentinel-Weave is a pluggable module. Users can enable, disable, or reconfigure individual components without touching the core. This means the framework adapts to your playstyle, not the other way around.

### 🖥️ Responsive Configuration Interface
The configuration surface is designed with responsiveness as a first-class principle. Whether displayed on a wide desktop monitor or a narrow side panel, the interface reflows cleanly and remains readable.

### 🌐 Multilingual Interface Support
Sentinel-Weave ships with localization scaffolding for multiple languages, allowing communities around the world to interact with the framework in their native tongue.

### 🕑 Round-the-Clock Support Cadence
Although Sentinel-Weave is a community-driven project, its support cadence is structured to feel continuous. Documentation, issue triage, and community assistance are organized around a rolling schedule so that no user is left stranded.

## 🧭 Design Philosophy

Sentinel-Weave was born from a simple observation: most combat utilities treat defense as an afterthought. They bolt parrying onto an offensive core and call it done. Sentinel-Weave inverts that relationship. Here, **defense is the core**, and offense is the extension.

This inversion produces a framework that feels fundamentally different to use. It is calmer. It is more deliberate. It rewards patience and positioning rather than raw input spam. In a combat environment saturated with noise, Sentinel-Weave is the quiet confidence in the corner.

The project's metaphor is weaving. Each defensive layer is a thread. Each thread interlocks with the next. Together they form a fabric that cannot be torn by a single strike.

## 🧱 Subsystem Breakdown

### The Parry Matrix
The parry matrix is the heart of Sentinel-Weave. It maintains a continuous scan of incoming threat vectors and calculates intercept opportunities in real time. Each threat is scored by proximity, velocity, and angle of approach, then routed to the appropriate defensive response.

### The Equip Lattice
The equip lattice handles emergency armament retrieval. It maintains a live inventory map of nearby equipment and selects the optimal candidate based on distance, type, and current defensive deficit.

### The Authority Core
The authority core governs offensive output. It ensures that attacks are issued with the correct timing, stance, and commitment level, and that no swing is wasted on a target outside viable range.

### The Harmony Layer
The harmony layer coordinates between subsystems. It prevents conflicts, resolves priority disputes, and ensures that defensive and offensive routines never step on each other's toes.

### The Telemetry Weave
The telemetry weave collects optional runtime metrics for self-diagnosis and tuning. It is entirely local and never transmits data externally.

## 🎨 User Experience Principles

Sentinel-Weave adheres to five UX principles:

1. **Invisibility by default.** The framework should never demand attention it does not need.
2. **Predictable response.** Every action the framework takes should be explainable.
3. **Configurable depth.** Beginners get sane defaults; veterans get full control.
4. **Graceful failure.** When something goes wrong, the framework steps aside rather than compounding the error.
5. **Respect for the player.** The human is always in command.
